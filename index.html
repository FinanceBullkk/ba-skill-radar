Methodology note: the seven skill axes are drawn from the international standard BABOK Guide v3 (IIBA) — covering 4 of 6 knowledge areas and 3 of 6 underlying competencies, the ones relevant to BAs working in a product support environment. The remaining two areas (Strategy Analysis, BA Planning & Monitoring) belong to the senior-BA level and sit on the phase 2 roadmap. Starting scores are inferred from the language of the TNA (e.g. "no consistent methodology or template" → 1.5; "has gained practical experience" → 3) and should be calibrated against team leads' assessments or a self-assessment survey. The default target is level 4 — works independently to a template — because every course module ends with a standard template plus hands-on practice using the team's real tickets. Any scores you adjust are saved automatically in this browser.
